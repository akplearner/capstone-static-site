## Exam coverage — 80% of the exam weight is practised

| Domain | Weight | Practised by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Context and scope (ISO 27001 cl. 4) | 20% | — Scope and the rules of engagement are set in the setup week; the graded weeks start from a signed scope |
| Leadership, planning and risk (cl. 5–6) | 20% | Risk assessment & Statement of Applicability; External attack-surface assessment; CIS baseline gap scan; Threat-model the proposed controls |
| Support, operation and controls (cl. 7–8, Annex A) | 20% | Build the control matrix; Implement the technical controls; Detection engineering |
| Performance evaluation (cl. 9) | 20% | Penetration test & retest; Coordinate validation & collect evidence; Report detection & response metrics; Run the internal audit; Assemble the audit evidence packet |
| Improvement (cl. 10) | 20% | Close out the retest and attest |
