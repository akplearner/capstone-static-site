## Exam coverage — 100% of the exam weight is practised

| Domain | Weight | Practised by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network Fundamentals | 20% | Discover before you design; Design the shape; Hand the network over |
| Network Access | 20% | Bring the first switches up; Cut the network into departments; Survive a cable being pulled; Put the WLANs on the air |
| IP Connectivity | 25% | Route between the departments; Join the second building; Work a ticket, not a hunch; Something breaks — work it properly |
| IP Services | 10% | One way out to the internet; Back it up, then change it properly; Build a small NOC |
| Security Fundamentals | 15% | Turn the policy into access lists; Harden the management plane |
| Automation and Programmability | 10% | A source of truth, not a spreadsheet; The network as data; Configure from the source of truth |
